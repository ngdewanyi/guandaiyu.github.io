I am an undergraduate in Accounting (ACCA Direction) at the College of Management, Ocean University of China, and a part-time research fellow at the Institute of Applied Accounting.

My research is empirical, examining how institutional arrangements—property-rights reform and strategic alliances—shape firm outcomes such as innovation, investment efficiency, and ESG performance in China.
