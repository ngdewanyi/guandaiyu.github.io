About

个人简介

管戴煜，男，中国海洋大学管理学院会计学（ACCA方向）本科生（2023级）。研究方向为公司治理、国有企业改革与ESG表现。中国海洋大学应用会计研究所兼职研究员，从事战略联盟网络与公司行为的实证研究。GRE: V 162 / Q 170 / AW 3.5。

I am an undergraduate student at Ocean University of China, majoring in Accounting (ACCA Direction) at the College of Management. My research interests lie in corporate governance, state-owned enterprise (SOE) reform, and ESG performance.

My work employs empirical methods to examine how institutional arrangements—such as property rights reform and strategic alliances—shape firm outcomes including innovation, investment efficiency, and sustainability practices.
